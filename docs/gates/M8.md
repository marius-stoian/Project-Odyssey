## Delegated technical choices made in M8 (Dominus)
The conversation panel is a screen of `RunFlow`; a conversation keeps a copy of its script (F5 cannot change words under the player); opening a conversation does not call the old `talkTo` (no free opinion from opening and closing the panel) except for generated small talk, which keeps the old warming; `mood()` is a condition function; the free-text memory (`MemoryNote`) is saved and hashed but never read by the simulation; `remember` defaults to feeling 10; the bubbles listen to the simulation (the chronicle and a small queue for `talk`) and never decide it; "near the camera" is within 12 m of the hero. See `docs/plans/US-161.md` to `US-165.md` and Milestone-68 to 73.
