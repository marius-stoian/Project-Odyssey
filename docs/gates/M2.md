## Recommendation (Mraw): **Go, if 2 of 3 readers find a story**
- Why go: the simulation is stable, fast (a century in under half a second), deterministic, saved safely, and it produces a family saga with famine, feuds and loss, all from data the owner can retune without code.
- If readers find it dry but see a story: go, and plan a small "why" layer for M3 (a reason in feud and death sentences, e.g. "over stolen food", "the hard winter of year 74").
- If fewer than 2 of 3 find a story: pivot, as the Codex's kill rule says: no new content; redesign what the chronicle tells (fewer, richer events with causes and named heroes) before M3.
