## The offline edit loop (the owner's way of working)
1. Edit `assets/data/interactions/tend-fire.json` (rename the label), save.
2. Press F5 in the game: the menu at the clan's fire shows the new name; a typo shows a red panel with `file:line: message` and nothing changes until it is fixed.

Shown by the tests above and described step by step in `docs/guides/interaction-data.md`, `docs/plans/stories-M7.md#us-152` and `US-156.md`.
