# US-181 Objectives from world events: plan and checks

Design: docs/plans/M10-quests-design.md section 4. Guide: docs/guides/quests.md ("What the game reports").

## Built
- The matching of every objective kind lives in `QuestBook` since US-180 (events are counted only after the step started; wait and flag need none).
- New reporting points, all hero-only: `ActionRunner::setFinishObserver` (interact), `HeroLife::setItemObserver` (gather, craft), `reportTalk` (conversation opened), `GameEffectHost` `take hero` (give), `OdysseyGame::strike` (defeat), `tickQuests` (goto, from the level places).
- `OdysseyGame::questEvent` stamps each event with the action clock.

## Manual checks (run at X-M10)
1. Quest `gather berries 3`: pick two bushes, the tracker (US-183) or the log shows progress 2/3; a third completes the step.
2. A clan member gathering berries (the camp) does not move the quest.
3. `talk elder`: opening the elder conversation completes it; walking away from a person who is not the elder does not.
4. `goto market`: with a place named Market in the level, standing near it completes the step.
5. `defeat goblin 2`: strike two goblins to the ground.
