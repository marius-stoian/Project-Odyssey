# US-182 Getting and handing in quests: plan and checks

Design: docs/plans/M10-quests-design.md section 5. Decisions: D-57 Q4, Q5.

## Built
- Effect verb `quest start|complete|fail <id>` carried out by the game (message "New quest: ..."), conditions `quest(id)` and `step(id)` in the game rule context.
- Quest fields `offer` and `turnIn`.
- `addQuestChoices`: a copy of the person script gets "Do you have work for me?" (offer, "I will do it." / "Not now.") and, for a quest whose active step is `talk <this person>`, the turn-in line first.
- `questSignFor` and `OdysseyGame::drawQuestSigns`: gold ! (quest to give) or ? (a quest wants a word) over people within 8 tiles.
- Any person who fits `giver` can give the quest (changed from "first living" in the design).

## Manual checks (run at X-M10)
1. Quest with `giver: "role:elder"`: the elder shows a !; Talk offers the work; "I will do it." starts it.
2. The last step `talk elder` with a `turnIn`: the elder shows a ?; talking plays the line and the reward arrives.
3. A `.dlg` choice with `{quest start first-day}` starts the quest too.
