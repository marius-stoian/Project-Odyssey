# D-67 US-206 design decisions (clans and people inspector)

Status: **Decided by Dominus (delegated)**, 2026-10-09, under D-41. The owner may override any line later; if one changes, US-206 is reopened. Design: docs/plans/M12-world-editing-design.md section 10.

| Q | Question | Options considered (recommended first) | Decision and reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Where the setup lives | The `clans` and `people` sections of the world file (brief 4.6) at the top level; inside the placed entries | **Top-level sections, as the brief says.** `clans` is keyed by "player" or a rival camp's name; `people` is keyed by a **clan member's number** (the people the clan starts with). A placed person is set by its own entry and properties (US-204), so there is one place per kind of person. |
| Q2 | What a clan sets | leader, stance, store, debts, partners, members | **All six are stored and checked.** Applied at the start: the player's **store of meals** (`food`), the player's **store items** and **debts to rivals** (in the hero's run), and each **rival's meals**. `leader`, `stance`, `partners` and `members` are stored and checked but nothing reads them until Politics (M13); `members` is a check (nobody in two clans), not a move. |
| Q3 | What a clan member sets | kin, opinions, grudges (items, routine, actions belong to placed people) | **Kin, opinions and grudges.** Opinions are set **as written** (-50 means -50, not "minus 50 more"). A grudge needs a weight (1 to 100) and a **reason**, and the chronicle writes the reason as an entry the grudge points to ("Brak holds a grudge against Tok: stole the last flint."), so the story engine can tell it. Kin sets mother, father and partner (the partner is set both ways). |
| Q4 | A placed person's items, routine, actions | Separate forms; properties of the entry | **Properties of the entry** (US-204's mechanism): `allow`, `deny` (interaction ids), `day`, `night` (the routine, the same text as the Editor's schedule form), `stock` (what it owns and trades), `does` (the actions it does on its own), next to `hp`, `swordDamage`, `family`, `attitude`, `tags`. Each is checked by the rules of the Editor's own fields, wins over the kind, and changes only that person. |
| Q5 | Inherited versus override display | Grey inherited values and a reset button; the properties as they are | **Left out.** An entry shows only what it overrides, and clearing one (`key=`) is the reset. The grey inherited column needs the kind data in the Region view and is not in the acceptance criteria; a new story if wanted. |
| Q6 | Consistency checks | A list; a block | **A list, never a block** (D-61 Q10): a clan that is not in the world, a debt or partner naming a clan that is not there, a member in two clans, a kin cycle, a partner who does not name them back, a person who is their own partner. |
| Q7 | Undo | Setup edits outside the history; one step each | **One step each**, in the same world history: a step holds the setup before and after. |
| Q8 | Where it reaches the game | Now; from US-207 | **From US-207, as D-63, D-65 and D-66.** `applyClanSetup`, `applyHeroSetup` and `applyRivalSetup` start a game from the setup and are tested; Play here (US-207) calls them. The clan's people exist only once a game starts, so the Editor asks for a member's **number** and cannot list names. |
| Q9 | Debts | A new clan-to-clan debt system; the hero's existing debts | **The hero's existing debts.** A debt of the player's clan to a rival is a `Debt` of the hero's run (goods, value, due day), the same one a barter on credit makes; a debt owed by a rival is stored and checked only. |
