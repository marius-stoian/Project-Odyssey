# D-GATE-M2b: Kill Gate 1 retry (owner, human gate)

**Status:** Open. Asked 2026-09-30 by Mraw at the X-M2b exit review. **The team waits for this answer before M3.**

**Question.** Is the printed story of the clan a story? (You judge alone, D-18.)

How to find out: read [the reader packet](../gates/M2b-reader-packet.md) (about 15 minutes): 40 named episodes, each with a beginning, a turn, an end and its people, then the births, deaths, pairings and feuds with their reasons. Or run it yourself on any seed: `odysseus_headless.exe --seed 3 --years 100 --story`.

## What changed since the first gate
Your notes were: not really a story; needs reasons for deaths and feuds, more interaction types, fewer more complex events. M2b answered each: every death and feud now says why (US-110); quarrels, blame, revenge and exile (US-111); sickness, nursing, sharing and adoption (US-112); courtship, rivals, jealousy and parting (US-113); apprentices, hunting parties, heroes, cowards and rescues (US-114); and the chronicle told as 40 episodes a century (US-115).

## Team recommendation
**Option B, Go with notes** if the episodes read as stories but feel repetitive. The honest weak spots we see: many episodes are hunts and hard winters (the clan's life has few other big themes yet); partings and rejections are rare; episodes about a feud can span ten years. Option A if you find it a story as it is. We would choose C only if the episodes read as lists of unrelated events.
