# D-02: Side Characters for Age 1: needs, traits, relationships (delegated to Dominus, 2026-09-30)

**Question.** The Side Characters pillar was never interviewed (OPEN-10, SDC-01 "not yet defined"). M2 (US-011..US-013) needs a minimal, playable definition. The requirements give hints: needs drive behaviour, "traits change behaviour", memories and gossip spread "slightly distorted" (GD-03), reputations and grudges create stories (5.x, Game Modes), the chronicle is the clan's tapestry (NA-02).

## Options considered

| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Small and legible (chosen)**: 4 needs (D-03), 6 traits, one opinion score per pair plus kinship, memories with feelings, gossip at half strength | Enough moving parts for stories to emerge; every rule fits on one page; the Kill Gate 1 test ("do readers find a story in the chronicle?") measures exactly this | Less depth than a Sims-style model; more traits later |
| B. Rich model now: Big-Five personality, multi-axis relationships (trust, affection, respect) | Deeper, more nuanced | Many untested knobs before the kill gate proves the idea; slower to balance |
| C. Wait for the owner's interview | The owner's own vision | Blocks M2; against the owner's instruction for minimal intervention |

## Decision (A)

**Needs** (D-03): Hunger, Energy, Warmth, Social; integers 0-100, 100 = fully satisfied. Each drops by a daily rate from `assets/data/sim/needs.json`; Warmth drops faster in winter. Hunger at 0 for 3 days, or Warmth at 0 for 3 winter days, kills.

**Traits**: each person has 1-2 of six, inherited from a parent with a chance of a new one:

| Trait | Effect on choices | Effect on stories |
|---|---|---|
| Brave | Hunting scores higher, even mammoths | First mammoth hunts; more hunting deaths |
| Timid | Avoids hunting; prefers gathering | Survives longer, is gossiped about less |
| Kind | Gives gifts and shares food | Good memories, friends |
| Greedy | Keeps food; sometimes steals from the store | Bad memories, grudges, feuds |
| Talkative | Talks more; gossip spreads further | Reputations travel |
| Diligent | Work actions score higher, rest lower | Feeds the clan in lean winters |

**Relationships**: every person has an opinion of every other person, -100..+100, plus kinship (parents, children, partner). Opinions change through memories: a gift +15, a shared meal +3, theft -25, a death of kin by someone's fault -40. Two adults with opinions above +60 of each other may pair; paired adults may have children. Opinions below -50 both ways start a feud, which the chronicle records.

**Memories and gossip (GD-03)**: a memory is who, what, when, a feeling (-100..+100) and an importance (minor or major). When two people talk, the speaker may pass on one memory the listener lacks, as a copy at half strength ("slightly weaker"). Minor memories are forgotten after 60 days; major ones (births, deaths, feuds, mammoth hunts, theft) are kept for life.

**Why A.** It is the smallest model that can produce the stories Kill Gate 1 asks readers to find (feuds, generous heroes, lean winters, first hunts), it is fully deterministic (integers, seeded random streams), and every number lives in `assets/data/sim/` so the owner can retune without code. The owner may override any part; an override is a new decision and changes data first, code second.

## Tuning note (2026-09-30, US-014, Dominus)
Balancing 12 simulated centuries showed that at +60 too few couples formed and clans died out, so unpaired adults now court (they prefer talking to possible partners) and pair at a mutual opinion of +50. All life numbers are data in `assets/data/sim/life.json`; the model itself (needs, traits, opinions, memories, gossip, pairing, feuds) is unchanged. See [the US-014 plan](../plans/stories-M2.md#us-014).
