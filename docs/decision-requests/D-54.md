## M9b Trade economy

| Q | Question | Owner answer |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Currency | Both coin items and an abstract balance. The owner marks which items act as currency per region (Editor, US-280), each with a value. Coin items in the clan stockpile convert to a balance when the trade screen opens; change is returned as coin |
| Q2 | Several currencies | Converted through item value: every item has a base value; any currency item is worth its value anywhere; traders price in their own region's currency. No exchange screen |
| Q3 | Pricing | All layers: region market base price per good, times the trader's stock-ratio curve (stock against target, clamped 50% to 200%), times a per-trade drift (each trade nudges a fixed percent, decaying back daily); reputation is applied last. All tunables in one JSON file; integer maths |
| Q4 | Reputation | The trader's M9a opinion of the player, in bands: hostile refuses to trade, wary +25% price, neutral base, friendly -10%, devoted -20% and rare stock unlocked |
| Q5 | Barter | A Haggle button: a seeded roll (world seed, deterministic) with odds from opinion and persuasion skill; one try per trader per in-game day; a failure costs opinion. A normal barter is accepted when the trader's received value is at least the given value at their prices, with a live balance bar |
| Q6 | Restock | A random weighted delivery each day from the trader's class goods list plus region resources; the Editor sets weights per good per trader or class. Stock is limited; wants at full value, other goods at half (D-52 Q-17) |
| Q7 | Who trades | Any NPC with a trade profile; the Trader class is only a default profile |
| Q8 | Whose stock | The player trades from and into the shared clan stockpile (as rival barter does today). Rival camp barter keeps working through the generalised screen |

## Technical choices made by Dominus under the owner's autonomy (recorded, the owner may override)
- **Codex amendment (Q11, Q14, Q15).** Anima masters the Codex on Drive; the repository copy is amended to v2.12 (change-log row, US-291 without the quest scenario, US-293 with the defaults files, US-294 with 30 days) and the issue is CI-013 in docs/codex-issues.md so Anima can adopt it.
- **Prices in item-value units.** Every price is computed in the value units of `assets/data/hero/items.json`; a currency item is an item with a value, so "paying in shells" pays value units in shell items. Format and formula: ADR-023.
- **Schedules are evaluated on the hour** (the same grid as needs); far persons follow them in the daily summary, spread over the day so that no tick pays for the whole population (ADR-022 addendum).
- **Combat abstraction.** NPC fights use the hero's weapon numbers (hit points and sword damage of the placed character) in the same strike-and-strike-back rounds, resolved by the simulation (headless); the Game only draws the result.
- **Persuasion skill** is the hero's Trade affinity divided by 10 (the five professions have no persuasion skill; Trade is the pillar that trade builds).
