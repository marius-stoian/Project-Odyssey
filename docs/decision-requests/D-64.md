# D-64 US-203 design decisions (water and mountains)

Status: **Decided by Dominus (delegated)**, 2026-10-09, under D-41. The owner may override any line later; if one changes, US-203 is reopened. Design: docs/plans/M12-world-editing-design.md section 6.

| Q | Question | Options considered (recommended first) | Decision and reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | How the tools are stored | Macros made of tile edits (one file format, one Undo, one regeneration rule); a new list of rivers and lakes in the world file | **Tile edits.** The Codex says "stored as overrides"; a river is the tiles it covers. Nothing new in the world file, so no new format. |
| Q2 | What a ford is | A strip of land across the whole width of the river (meadow tiles over the water), every N tiles along the river (field "Fords", 0 = none), plus a Ford tool that turns the water under the brush into land; a new "ford" biome that is shallow water | **A strip of land.** "Walking and shot blocking follow the existing solid rules": the game knows solid water and walkable ground, and a new biome would need new tile art, rules and saves. |
| Q3 | Shapes | River and ridge: the four-connected line from source to mouth, drawn from the earlier end so it is the same whichever way it is dragged, thickened with the round brush stamp (width = brush size); lake: a disc whose radius is the distance dragged | **As listed.** Four-connected so nobody squeezes through a corner; whole numbers only (Charter rule 6). |
| Q4 | Cave mouths | Only into a mountain tile (a cliff), refused with a message elsewhere; anywhere | **Into a cliff only.** That is the story ("a cliff, place a cave mouth"). In the game a Cave tile is the path tile: not solid, so it can be entered. |
| Q5 | Removing water | A Dry tool that makes the connected water (a lake or a river) meadow, as edits over the seed (the same fill as Fill, with the same 20,000 tile limit and the edge wall left alone) | **Dry tool.** The change is an override and one step of Undo. |
| Q6 | Reachability | A warning line in the Region view (the start has become water or lost its water and food, a cave mouth with no walkable tile next to it); never a block | **Warning, as D-61 Q10 decided.** The owner may want an island. |
| Q7 | The smooth-shore pass (design section 6) | Build it; leave it out | **Left out.** It is not in the acceptance criteria and the scope rule says anything else is a new story. The design section is amended. |
| Q8 | Where the settings panel sits | Under the second row of tools, smaller | **Moved down and made smaller** (rows of 14 pixels, 92 pixel maps, one conflict line) so the tool row does not cover it. |
