# D-55 M8d and M8e kickoff answers: buildings and building life

Status: **Decided (owner, 2026-10-05)**, at the kickoff of M8d and M8e. Related: D-42, D-43, D-06. Design: docs/plans/M8d-buildings-design.md and docs/plans/M8e-building-life-design.md. Test cadence (owner, same day): tests are written with each story and compile; the one full verify (Debug and Release) runs at the end of each milestone, X-M8d and X-M8e (as D-53 and D-54 did).

## M8d Buildings

| Q | Question | Owner answer |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Build menu layout | A side list panel: a scrollable list with icon, name and material cost; click to place |
| Q2 | Blueprints known at the start | Hut and windbreak only; the other kinds are learned later (unlock hook kept simple: a `known` flag in the hero's save, set by data) |
| Q3 | Blueprint look before the materials arrive | A translucent ghost until the building is complete; then the finished building appears (no stage art) |
| Q4 | Where whole blueprints may be placed | The hero places anywhere valid: flat, free, not on water or solid ground; 1 m grid; 90 degree turns; an invalid spot is shown red |

## M8e Building life

| Q | Question | Owner answer |
|---|---|---|
| Q5 | Wear speed | Very slow: wear is almost off; buildings are damaged only by events (fire, raids, storms) |
| Q6 | Rivals and your buildings | Rivals may damage or burn buildings only at war or feud |
| Q7 | Interior maps by default | No kind opens an interior map by default: every roofed kind fades its roof; the Editor can set a map per kind or prefab |
| Q8 | Fire | Spreads from piece to piece by material (wood burns, mud and stone resist, rain slows it); the clan fetches water and the hero can douse it |
