# D-17: movement directions (delegated to Dominus, 2026-09-30)

**Question.** US-024 says "move my character in four directions with animation", but the owner decided 8 facing directions for sprites (D-04). Which movement should Luna and the demo support?

| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| **8-way movement, diagonals at the same speed (chosen)** | Uses the 8 facing directions the owner chose; feels natural with a gamepad stick | Diagonal art frames needed (placeholder art covers them) |
| 4-way movement, 8 facing directions for idle poses only | Matches US-024's wording exactly | The 8-direction art would mostly go unused |

**Decision.** 8-way movement. US-024's three scenarios (walk right, stop at a rock, stop and face the last direction) are unchanged and still tested. The owner may override; switching to 4-way is a one-line change in the movement code.
